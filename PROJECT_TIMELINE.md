### ⏳ PHASE 2: INCUBATION ("The Silent Failure")

**April 2024 — November 2025**
*   **📉 The Vacuum Effect:**
    *   Ridge vent pulls air; lack of soffit intake creates negative pressure.
    *   Warm, moist air is sucked from the living space into the cold attic.
    *   **Result:** Mold begins to grow on the underside of the *new* plywood during winter months.
