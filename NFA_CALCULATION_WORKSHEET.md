## C. BALANCE ANALYSIS ✅ CALCULATED

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Total Exhaust (A)** | **1,080 sq. in.** |
| **Total Intake (B)** | 600 + 100 = **700 sq. in.** |
| **Deficit (A - B)** | **380 sq. in.** |
| **Deficit Percentage** | **35%** |

### Conclusion
- [ ] **BALANCED:** Intake ≥ Exhaust (System compliant)
- [x] **IMBALANCED:** Exhaust > Intake (Depressurization / "Vacuum Effect")

> [!WARNING]
> **Result:** The attic is operating at a 35% intake deficit, creating negative pressure that pulls warm, moist air from the living space into the cold attic — causing condensation and mold growth.
