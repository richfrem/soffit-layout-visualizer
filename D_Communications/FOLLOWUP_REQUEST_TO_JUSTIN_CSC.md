## 1. Written Defect Report

### Defects Observed
- The **ventilation imbalance** (1,080 sq. in. exhaust vs. ~700 sq. in. intake = 35% deficit)
- The **SV-50 vents** are positioned on the lower roof, not at the soffits
- **Lack of proper soffit intake** at the eaves (only 4 small vents on E/W, zero on N/S)
- The **moisture/mold conditions** resulting from the "Vacuum Effect"

### Building Code & Building Science Justification

Please reference why the SV-50s should be removed:

**BC Building Code 9.19.1.3:**
> Requires a 63mm (2.5 inch) airspace between insulation and roof sheathing at the wall plates (eaves). The current SV-50 configuration does not provide intake at the eave level, creating a "dead zone" that violates this requirement.

**Manufacturer Specification (Menzies SV-50):**
> The product page states: *"Exhaust vent for mounting at upper portion of steep roof. Not to be used as an intake vent."* The SV-50s were incorrectly specified as "air intake vents" in the original roofing quote.

**Building Science Principle (e.g. Lstiburek):**
> Balanced ventilation requires intake at the lowest point (soffits) and exhaust at the highest point (ridge). "Wash the entire underside of the roof deck with cold air." The current configuration creates short-circuiting.
