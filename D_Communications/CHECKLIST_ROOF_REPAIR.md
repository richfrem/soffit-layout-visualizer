**Focus:** Waterproofing & Structural Integrity

## 🛑 STOP POINTS (Inspect Before They Proceed)

### 1. 🔍 The Patch Product (Decide: Plug vs. Wood)
*Ask at 9 AM: "Are we doing the Metal Smart Plug or a Wood Patch?"*

**IF METAL SMART PLUG:**
- [ ] **Brand Check:** Is it a branded product (e.g., *Smart Plug*, *Kwik-Plug*)?
- [ ] **Stiffness Check:** Is it rigid 24-gauge steel (rated for 700 lbs)? Don't accept flimsy sheet metal.
- [ ] **Material:** OSB bonded to a galvanized steel flange.

**IF CUSTOM WOOD PATCH ("Plywood Square"):**
- [ ] **⚠️ STRUCTURAL CHECK:** They **MUST install 2x4 wooden blocking** inside the hole to support the new plywood.
    - *Ask:* "Are you putting blocking between the trusses?"
    - *Reject:* Do not accept a piece of plywood just nailed to the edges without support. It will sag.

### 2. 📸 The Open Hole (The "Tuck" Check)
*Ask them to show you (or take a photo of) the first hole prep.*
- [ ] **Shingle Removal:** Have they removed enough shingles to expose the deck?
- [ ] **Underlayment Lift:** IMPORTANT. Have they carefully lifted the *upper* row of existing underlayment/felt?
    - *Reason: The new patch flange must slide UNDER the existing paper to shed water correctly.*

### 3. 🛠️ The Installation
*Watch one install or inspect before shingling.*
- [ ] **Flush Fit:** Is the metal flange hammered completely flat to the deck?
    - *Risk: Raised nails or bent metal will poke through the paper.*
- [ ] **Fasteners:** Are they using nails (not staples) to secure the flange?
- [ ] **The "Sandwich":**
    1.  Calculated hole cut.
    2.  Patch installed.
    3.  **Ice & Water Shield (or new synthetic paper)** installed OVER the patch.
    4.  **TUCKED UNDER** the existing paper row above.

### 4. 🧱 The Finish
- [ ] **Shingle Match:** Are the new shingles a good color match?
- [ ] **Flatness:** Does the repaired area lay flat? (No humps or bumps).
