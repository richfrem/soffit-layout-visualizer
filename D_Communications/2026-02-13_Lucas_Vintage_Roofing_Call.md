### 🛠 Phase 2: Structural Roof Deck Patching (Vintage Roofing)
**Objective:** Seal the resulting holes structurally and watertight.
- **Method:** Prefabricated roof hole patches (Industry Std: *Kwik-Plug* or *Smart Plug* — **User to confirm brand with Lucas**).

- **Application:** Dropped in from the roof exterior; flange nailed flush to the deck.

- **Advantage:** Eliminates need for custom internal wood blocking; provides a structural, flush surface for new underlayment and shingles.
