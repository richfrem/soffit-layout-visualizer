## DISCLAIMER CLAUSES MARK MAY CITE

### Clause 1: Pre-Existing Conditions
> *"Vintage Roofing LTD disclaims any and all responsibility for any pre-existing damage and conditions, including but not limited to clogged drains, **mold**, structural damage or deficiencies..."*

**Mark's Argument:** "The mold was pre-existing."

**YOUR REBUTTAL:**
*   The mold is on **NEW plywood installed in April 2024**.
*   Date stamps on plywood prove the material didn't exist before Mark's work.
*   Therefore, the mold cannot be "pre-existing" — it developed AFTER installation.

---

### Clause 2: Hidden Defects
> *"It is hereby agreed that this contractor will not be held responsible for damage to or repairs to the property as a result of hidden defects, deterioration, rot, or any **non-compliance with the local building codes inherent at the time**."*

**Mark's Argument:** "The ventilation issue was inherent to the structure."

**YOUR REBUTTAL:**
*   The non-compliance was **CREATED** by Mark's installation, not "inherent."
*   Mark installed a new Omni Ridge Vent and SV-50s — a new system, not an existing one.
*   The imbalance (1,080 exhaust vs. 700 intake) is a defect of the NEW configuration.
*   BC Building Code 9.19.1.2 violations were introduced by his work.

---

### Clause 3: Existing Defects
> *"Vintage Roofing LTD shall not be responsible for any damage due to causes beyond the control of Vintage Roofing, including **existing defects to the structure**."*

**Mark's Argument:** "The soffit vents were not my responsibility."

**YOUR REBUTTAL:**
*   The SV-50 vents were Mark's installation choice.
*   Using exhaust-rated vents (SV-50) as intake is a defect HE CREATED.
*   Menzies explicitly states: "Not to be used as an intake vent."
*   The ventilation system design was within Mark's control.

## CLAUSES THAT SUPPORT YOUR CLAIM

### Building Code Compliance
The contract does not include a waiver of building code compliance for NEW WORK performed. The disclaimers only cover conditions "inherent at the time" — not new defects introduced by the contractor.

### Professional Standard of Care
Even without explicit warranty language, contractors in BC have an implied duty to perform work in a workmanlike manner according to industry standards.
