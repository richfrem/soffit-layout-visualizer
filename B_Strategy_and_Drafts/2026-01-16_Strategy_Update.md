## 1. Executive Summary

The "Vacuum Effect" diagnosis (Attic Depressurization) has been stress-tested against local industry feedback (Parker Johnston), broader industry consensus (Reddit), **expert building science forensics**, and now **verified against Vintage Roofing's own invoice and manufacturer specifications**.

## 4. THE CORE ISSUE: Failure to Advise

> [!CAUTION]
> **Mark never presented you with a compliant option or allowed you to make an informed decision.**

### What Should Have Happened

A professional roofer should have said:

> *"These SV-50s should provide sufficient intake — everyone uses them even though they aren't supported for intake by the manufacturer. It's common practice. However, if you want to be fully compliant with building code and have proper 1:1 ventilation balance, you would need a stucco/soffit contractor to install continuous soffit vents. That would cost approximately $X versus this option at $Y. Which would you prefer?"*

### What Actually Happened

*   Mark installed SV-50s labeled as "intake vents" **without disclosure** that:
    1. The manufacturer says they're **not for intake use**
    2. The configuration creates a **35% ventilation deficit**
    3. Compliant soffit venting was an available alternative
*   You were **never given the choice** between "common practice" and "code compliant"
*   Had you been informed, you could have paid for proper soffit vents upfront — **avoiding mold, remediation costs, and this entire dispute**

### This Is Professional Negligence

| Duty | Failure |
|------|---------|
| **Duty to Disclose** | Did not explain SV-50s are rated "exhaust only" |
| **Duty to Advise** | Did not present the compliant soffit vent option |
