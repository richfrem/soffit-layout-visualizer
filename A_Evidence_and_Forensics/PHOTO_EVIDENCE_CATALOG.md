## KEY OBSERVATIONS

1. **Soffit Intake (Eave):** 4 small rectangular vents = **~100 sq. in. total**
   * **East Side (2 Vents):** Covers Master Bedroom, Ensuite, Main Bath, and two other Bedrooms. This critical area (especially over the moisture-producing bathrooms) is severely under-ventilated.
   * **West Side (2 Vents):** Covers Garage, Family Room, and Kitchen. Also under-ventilated, particularly over the Kitchen.
2. **SV-50 Mid-Roof:** 12 box vents (visible on the satellite map and photos) × 50 NFA = **600 sq. in.** ⚠️ **MISUSED AS INTAKE**
3. **Ridge Exhaust (Omni):** ~60 ft × 18 NFA = **~1,080 sq. in.**
4. **North/South Sides:** **Zero intake ventilation** (Covering Living Room/Dining Area/Patio and Garage Entrance/Master Bedroom Window respectively).
5. **Result:** Exhaust (1,080) >> Intake (700) → **~35% Deficit = Vacuum Effect**
   * The large, complex roof structure visible in the satellite map requires significantly more intake ventilation than currently exists to properly sweep the massive volume of attic air.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Menzies SV-50 manufacturer states:** "Not to be used as an intake vent."
