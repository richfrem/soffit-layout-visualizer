## 1. General Spacing Rules

*   **Default Spacing (Long Runs):** Place lights **6 to 8 feet apart** along the long East/West walls and non-feature areas. This prevents over-illumination on a single-story home (modern LED wafers at 700-1000 lumens each are very bright — too many will make the house glow like a commercial building).
*   **Feature Spacing (Focal Points):** Tighten to **4 to 5 feet apart** at the front entry, over the garage doors, and around exterior doors where you want a welcoming, well-defined wash of light.
*   **Distance from the Wall:** For your specific **22-24 inch wide soffits**, place them exactly **halfway out (11 to 12 inches from the wall)**. This centers the fixture in the soffit panel and creates a soft, wide "scallop" of light. Placing them too close to the wall creates a harsh hotspot and highlights every texture/imperfection in the stucco.
*   **Corners:** Always place a light **12 to 18 inches from the outside corners** of the roofline. This anchors the lighting design and defines the shape of the house at night.

## 2. Strategic Placement Areas

Instead of measuring a fixed distance blindly, adjust the spacing to align with these architectural features:

*   **Over Windows & Doors:** Center a light directly over the midpoint of large windows or doors to highlight them.
*   **Garage Doors:** Place a light centered over each side of the garage door frame, and one in the middle, to wash the doors in light.
*   **Front Entry:** Use a slightly tighter spacing (e.g., 3-4 feet) or a brighter setting near the front door to create a welcoming focal point.

## 3. Wall-by-Wall Breakdown

Based on the dimensions from the floorplan:

### Front of House (South) — *Zone 1 (Feature Spacing: 4-5 ft)*
*   **Garage Face (20'2" wide):** 4 to 5 lights @ 4-5 ft spacing. One near each corner, evenly spaced over the garage door.
*   **Main Entry Area (U-Shaped Recessed Foyer):** 
    *   *Dimensions:* Back wall = 100", Left wall (Garage side) = 111", Right wall (Master side) = 76". Soffit width = 23".
    *   *Back Wall (100" span):* 2 lights spaced 48-60" apart, symmetrically framing the door (approx 20-26" from corners). Aim gimbals slightly towards the door.
    *   *Left Wall (111" span):* 2 lights spaced ~55" apart to evenly wash the longer garage return.
    *   *Right Wall (76" span):* 1 light perfectly centered (~38" from the back corner).
    *   *Depth Constraint:* Place all entry lights dead-center at 11.5" from the wall in the 23" wide soffit.
*   **Master Bedroom Face (14'8" wide):** 2 to 3 lights. One near each corner, one centered over the window.

### East Wall (Master, Baths, Bedrooms) — *Zone 2 (Default Spacing: 6-8 ft)*
*   **Length (Approx. 50-60 ft):** 7 to 9 lights @ 7 ft spacing.
*   *Strategy:* Start at the front corner, end at the back corner, and space evenly at ~7 feet. Tweak slightly to center over the bedroom and bathroom windows.

### West Wall (Garage, Family Room, Kitchen) — *Zone 2 (Default Spacing: 6-8 ft)*
*   **Length (Approx. 50-60 ft):** 7 to 9 lights @ 7 ft spacing.
*   *Strategy:* Mirror the East wall. Tweak to center over the kitchen sink window and family room windows.

> [!NOTE] 
> **Decision: 360-Degree Security Lighting**
> You have confirmed the strategy to wrap the entire house (including non-walkway sides) with lighting. This is highly recommended for **security** (eliminates dark hiding spots), **visual balance** (prevents a lopsided look), and **interior ambiance** (stops windows from looking like black mirrors at night). The 6-8 ft default spacing handles this perfectly.

### Rear of House (North) — *Zone 3 (Mixed Spacing)*
*   **Living/Dining Area (Approx. 15-20 ft):** 3 to 4 lights @ 6 ft spacing.
*   **Patio (20'x17'):** 4 to 5 lights @ 4-5 ft spacing for evening entertaining task lighting. *(Note: Only a single row of lights is required here, matching the rest of the house, despite any AI mockup anomalies near the chimney).*

**Total Estimated Lights:** ~25 to 30 fixtures.

## 4. Hardware Recommendations

*   **Type:** 4-inch Round LED Wafer Lights (**Fixed** downlights preferred over gimbal for better value). Wafers are incredibly thin and don't require large metal cans in the attic.
*   **Fixture Finish (Trim Color):** Based on Justin's recommendation, **Black trims** are an excellent choice. 
*   **Color Temperature:** **2500K to 3000K (Warm White)**. Modern lights can easily be adjusted during or after installation.
*   **Lumen Range:** 600-800 lumens per fixture is ideal. Avoid 1000+ lumen fixtures.

## 5. Cedar Material Profile
*   **Board Width:** Justin recommends a **1x4 or 1x6 profile** for the cedar planks. The choice is purely aesthetic, though the board size will influence the exact dimension of the pot light trim we select to ensure varying widths don't clash.

## 5. Switch Zoning Strategy

Do **not** wire all lights to a single switch. Break the house into three independently controlled zones:

| Zone | Area | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| **Zone 1** | Front of House (Garage, Entry, Master Face) | Welcoming curb appeal — on at dusk, dim after 10 PM |
| **Zone 2** | East & West Walls (Side Yards) | Security — can run at low brightness all night |
| **Zone 3** | Rear (Living/Dining, Patio) | Entertaining — full brightness on demand |

*   **Smart Control:** Since you already use **Lutron smart switches** inside the house and for the main entrance, simply expand your existing Lutron ecosystem (e.g., Lutron Caseta Diva or Claro smart dimmers) to control these 3 new exterior zones. This allows seamless integration, scheduling (e.g., Zone 1 sunset to 10 PM), and unified control from your existing Lutron app.
