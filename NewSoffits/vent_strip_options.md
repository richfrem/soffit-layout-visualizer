## 🔍 Visualizer Alignment & Notes

### 📐 layout-visualizer Config Matching:
*   **Menzies Metal (Plastic)** matches your current app **1.75" setup** dimension edge-for-edge perfectly.
*   **Precision Metals (Aluminum)** will add an extra **0.25" overlap** to courses if swapped, as it sports a 2" exposed grid face faceplate.

### 🪵 Framing/Installation impact:
1.  **Thickness Clearance:**
    *   *Menzies:* at **1/4" deep**, fits completely flush inside standard 3/8" or 1/2" cedar profiles directly.
    *   *Precision:* at **3/4" deep**, requires significant backing recess or clearance gap behind the boards so it doesn't push the assembly away from the rafters.
2.  **Run rate efficiency:**
    *   Precision's **10ft units** require fewer joint breaks across a 238ft house perimeter setup than Menzies' **8ft models**.

---

## ❄️ Durability & Climate Resilience

*   🛡️ **Precision Metals (Aluminum):** **Highly Recommended for Freeze-Thaw Climates.** Aluminum handles expansion and contraction without becoming brittle. 
*   ⚠️ **Menzies (Plastic):** Shrines can become rigid and **prone to cracking** over repeated winter-to-summer temperature cycling if screwed down too tightly without movement gaps.
