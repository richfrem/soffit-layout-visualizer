### ⚠️ Considerations
1.  **Grade Confirmation (C&Btr):** Double-check that it is strictly **C & Better (C&Btr)** grading, just like West Wind. This guarantees knot-free, premium clear quality with no bleeding or sap issues.
2.  **No V-Groove Profile:** 
    *   Standard wood soffits often use V-groove to hide the natural expansion/contraction of wood with the seasons.
    *   Without a V-groove, you get a solid wall look. **Make sure you like this aesthetic** (looks more like modern paneling/flush deck) vs the traditional slatted ceiling look.
3.  **Actual Moisture Reading (16%):** User test of one piece came back above the 12% claim (ideal range for exterior is < 15%). **User Note:** 16% is likely too wet for oil finishes to penetrate well; might require additional drying time before finishing. **Note:** Tested piece was on top/outside edge; wood inside the stack will likely be wetter.
