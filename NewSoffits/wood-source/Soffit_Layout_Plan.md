# Soffit Layout & Board Selection (Vent Edition)

Factoring in a **1.75 inch continuous vent strip** down the center changes the amount of wood needed to cover your **22" to 24"** depths.

### 📐 Dimensional Math
Here is how different board widths sum up when combined with your **1.75" vent**:

#### 1. Using 1x4 Boards (3.25" Actual Face Reveal)
*   *Note: Nominal 1x4 has a 3.5" face, losing ~1/4" to the T&G locking joint.*
*   **6 Boards:** 6 × 3.25" = 19.5" + 1.75" Vent = **21.25 inches** (Short for 22" depth).
*   **7 Boards (Excel Row 1):** 7 × 3.25" = 22.75" + 1.75" Vent = **24.5 inches**
    *   *Best fit for 24" depth* (requires minor ripping of 1/2" off one edge course).

#### 2. Using 1x5 Boards (4.25" Actual Face Reveal)
*   *Note: Nominal 1x5 has an actual width of 4.5", losing ~1/4" to the joint.*
*   **5 Boards (Excel Row 24):** 5 × 4.25" = 21.25" + 1.75" Vent = **23.00 inches exactly.**
    *   *The Absolute Winner layout for a 23" depth with zero waste.*

---

#### 3. Using 1x6 Boards (5.0" to 5.25" Actual Face Reveal)
*   *Based on West Wind's 1x6 (5.25" width):* Assume **5.0" reveal** after joint.
*   **4 Boards:** 4 × 5.0" = 20.0" + 1.75" Vent = **21.75 inches**
*   **5 Boards:** 5 × 5.0" = 25.0" + 1.75" Vent = **26.75 inches** (Requires heavy rip-cutting).

## 🛠️ Aesthetic/Design Suggestion
Since you want the vent strip running **perfectly down the center**:
*   **1x4 or 1x6 (Even numbers of boards):** You place **equal boards** on each side of the vent. 
    *   *Example (1x6):* [ 2 Boards ] + [ Vent ] + [ 2 Boards ]. This creates perfect symmetry.
*   **1x5 (Odd number of boards):** Submitting 5 boards creates 2.5 boards on each side. One board will need to be ripped down the middle to hug the vent equally.

**The Verdict:** 
For symmetry and speed, an **even number of boards** (1x4 or 1x6) makes it easier to center the vent perfectly without cutting a board right down the core. 
The **1x6 with 4 boards** gives you a highly premium speed and symmetrical layout.

## 🌬️ Vent Placement: Outer Edge vs Center
You noted that building science suggests placing vents towards the outer gutter edge for maximum intake flow.

#### **Option A: 1-Board Deep (Fascia Side - Matches your Excel Worksheet)**
*   **The Layout:** `[Fascia]` ➡️ `[1 Board course]` ➡️ `[1.75" Vent]` ➡️ `[Remaining Wood]` ➡️ `[Stucco]`
*   *Note: Your Excel Row 1 (Segment 1 = 3") and Row 19 (Segment 1 = 3.25") are built exactly on this professional standard.*
*   **The Benefit:** Having **1 full board on the outer edge** creates a solid continuous frame that anchors safely against the fascia/gutter bracket, while keeping the air-vent close to the cold intake breeze.
*   **Aesthetic:** Looks like a framed border rather than an edge-split.

#### **Option B: Center Placement**
*   **The Layout:** `[2 Boards]` ➡️ `[Vent]` ➡️ `[2 Boards]`
*   **The Benefit:** Perfectly symmetrical, breaks up the flat ceiling nicely.
