## 1. THE CORE MISSION

A new roof installed in April 2024 caused severe attic depressurization ("The Vacuum Effect"), leading to rapid mold growth on brand-new 3/8" plywood.

### The "Belt and Suspenders" Fix
1. **BELT (Seal):** Foam-seal the attic floor (Advance Insulation)
2. **SUSPENDERS (Ventilate):** Cut new continuous soffit vents (Todd/Parker Johnston)
3. **CORRECTION:** Remove non-compliant SV-50 vents (Vintage Roofing warranty claim)

---

## 2. THE TECHNICAL FAILURE

| Component | Installed | NFA | Problem |
|-----------|-----------|-----|---------|
| **Exhaust** | Omni Ridge Vent | ~1,080 sq. in. | High capacity, creates suction |
| **Intake** | SV-50 Box Vents | ~600 sq. in. | Undersized, **mid-roof location** |
| **Deficit** | — | **45%** | Vacuum pulls moist air from house |

**The "Short Circuit":** SV-50s placed mid-roof leave the bottom 4 feet (eaves) with zero airflow — a "Dead Zone" violating BC Building Code 9.19.1.3.
